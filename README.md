# JSoundExplorer
Sound Explorer for Minecraft sounds, natively [JMHScript](https://jruk8.github.io/JManhunt/configuration/modifiers/tags-advanced/#messages-and-sounds) compatible.
Partly inspired by [mudkjp's](https://github.com/mudkjp/minecraft-sound-explorer).

## Key features
- » Play every Minecraft sound from the live catalog, with search and namespace filters
- » Click to play, double-click to copy the sound label to the clipboard
- » Pitch and volume sliders that retune live, plus most/least-played sorting
- » Copy-ready **JMHScript** snippet for the latest played sound
- » **Surprise me!** spotlights a random sound, and **Swipe!** turns discovery into a card game
- » **Play Classical!** performs bundled MIDI pieces on any sound from your history
- » Anonymous global play counts with hourly, daily, and monthly trend charts
